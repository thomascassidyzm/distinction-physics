# The shared root — what the distinction thesis and Configuration Economics actually have in common

*A canonical node, first articulation. Written 2026-09-06 from a live read of both repos, this one and `~/configuration-economics`. Nothing here is ruled. It is built for Tom's redline, not for publication. Everything quoted is Tom's own text or the two repos' own text; connective prose is marked [ASSEMBLER]. Where the two branches would disagree, the disagreement is stated and a position is taken, marked as mine.*

*Status: FORMING. The fork named in §4.3 is CANON C4, open since 2026-07-14 and still unruled.*

---

## 1. Why this node exists

The distinction thesis and Configuration Economics have been described as two branches from one root since CANON was assembled in July. CANON §4 says "both branches grow from least-action-under-finite-budget" and then moves on to who owns what. CE's lineage page says DP is "preferred vocabulary rather than required grounding." CE's keystone proposition restates DP §4.2 almost line for line. CE's observer-architecture note says DP's primitives are "the OLU conditions stated natively." Least-Time Learning puts an ethics leg on DP's front page that is CE's subject matter.

Four documents, four different accounts of the relationship, and the root itself never written down once. This is that statement.

## 2. The root, stated once

A bounded observer with a sense of persistence holds distinctions. Holding one costs energy, and holding it over time costs energy over time. The observer's budget is finite. So every pattern of distinctions the observer maintains is a configuration with a running cost, every change of configuration is a path with a cost, and nothing outside physics selects the cheap path for free. The work, whether it is called learning or intelligence or economics, is finding the cheap path and paying to hold what it reaches, against a floor that does not move.

That is the whole of it. Everything both branches share is in that paragraph. Everything either branch adds is downstream of it.

Pulled apart into its parts, with where each part is stated in each repo:

**The observer is bounded and persistent.** DP: Axiom 2, "all observers have finite energy budgets," and the OLU four-tuple. CE: `observer-relative-option-space` names the value-bearing class as "life-persisting observers — those that maintain themselves and make distinctions," and the observer-architecture note builds its whole argument on "computationally bounded" plus "a sense of persistence over time." [ASSEMBLER] The same two properties, named the same way in both. This is the deepest shared commitment and the one neither repo says is shared.

**A configuration is a pattern of distinctions, and it decays unless paid for.** DP: "no distinction is maintained indefinitely without expenditure," the Dynamism principle, `docs/derivations/effective-discreteness.md` §5 and the decay chain. CE: `complexity-maintenance`, `held-value-not-reachable`, and the essay's line that "complexity maintenance rises as the exergy inheritance shrinks." [ASSEMBLER] CE's maintenance cost is DP's decay theorem with the units changed from bits to exergy.

**Change is a path with a cost, and the cost is integrated over time.** DP: §4.2, "any learning trajectory a natural cost functional. That functional has units of energy × time." CE: the keystone, "every change of configuration is a trajectory through a space of distinctions, and its cost is the action S = ∫E dt." CE's `option-space-formalisation` makes the dual explicit: the reachable set R(C, B, T) "is precisely" the forward dual of DP's trajectory cost functional.

**Nothing selects the cheap path for free, except in physics.** DP §4.2: "Nature selects via δS = 0 as a law of motion. In learning, the trajectory is chosen by the pedagogy designer, or emerges from the learner's own allocation of attention." CE keystone: "among configurations that do not yet exist, nothing selects it automatically; the low-action path has to be searched for. That search is what pattern-intelligence is." [ASSEMBLER] This is the one place CE quotes DP rather than converging on it, and it is also the one place the two texts are word-for-word the same idea.

**The denominator does not move.** DP: Module 7, "heat death is the state without OLUs, where no distinctions are maintainable," and the ethics leg of the trinity, "selection over configurations, denominated against heat death." CE: `binding-constraint`, "the only true walls" are the physical invariants, "a conservation law, the entropy floor, an irreversibility on the relevant horizon," and the viable objective's refusal of any move that "asymptotically empties" the reachable set. [ASSEMBLER] Same wall, seen from the two sides of it. DP names it as the end of observation; CE names it as the end of reachability. Both refuse to let it be priced.

## 3. What each branch adds

### 3.1 What the distinction thesis adds and CE does not carry

- **The primitive, and its transcendental priority.** Distinction comes before any premise, because stating a premise is making a distinction. CE never uses this. CE's two `established` nodes, `exergy-not-energy` and `coordination-bounds-reachability`, stand on classical thermodynamics and observed enclosure with no observer theory in them, by design.
- **The theorem.** Effective discreteness, N ≤ 2^(E / k_B T ln 2), conditional on a stated observer model. CE has no theorem and does not claim one.
- **The resolution trade-off and the decay chain.** Barrier height, not cumulative spend, buys retention. CE inherits the consequence as maintenance cost but not the mechanism.
- **The learner as the home observer.** DP's cost functional is first-person: one learner's effort over their own seconds. Least-Time Learning prices three terms, the charge, the idle charge and the debt, and states that the weights come from telemetry, not the armchair.
- **The ruling that the quantity is time, not action.** "Least time in some ways is more apt than least action... time is not smuggling in any confusions, unlike action as a term with a distinct meaning in physics." Fermat, not Hamilton. See §4.1, because CE has not made this move.
- **The ruling that ontology is itself an OLU construct.** CANON C1, 2026-07-14: "helpful >> true... ontology ITSELF collapses under the distinction-is-primitive framework." See §4.2, because CE's formal definition of reachability presupposes what this ruling removes.
- **Scaling as the primitive under number.** Comparison is a concept; the numeral and the number line are facts. CE has no need of this and does not reference it.

### 3.2 What Configuration Economics adds and DP does not carry

- **Direction.** DP's functional is a cost looking backward along a path already taken or being chosen. CE turns it around: value is the forward reachable set, "configurations that expand future option space under bounded energy flux." DP has no theory of value at all; it has a denominator and a cost.
- **Observer-relativity of value, with a principled class.** "Option space for whom" gets a structural answer: the class of life-persisting observers. DP's OLU is one observer; CE's R_living is a class of them, which is where the aggregation problem of §4.5 enters.
- **The no-regret objective over moves, not a scalar over states.** "Prefer moves that keep R_living open for every major class of life and every relevant horizon; reject moves that asymptotically empty it." CE explicitly declined the scalar maximum. DP's learning theory has not yet decided whether its own extremal is a minimum or merely stationary; CE's ethics has already refused the question.
- **The binding constraint is sinks, materials and biosphere, not energy influx.** Solar exergy at 175 PW against 18 TW dissipated. DP's Axiom 2 is a finite budget with nothing said about where the bind actually falls. CE's move of the wall off energy is its own finding.
- **Exergy, not energy.** DP counts energy per bit. CE counts work-capable energy over a horizon. The two are the same in a Landauer calculation and not the same in an economy.
- **Money as a claim, not as action.** "Money is neither action nor pattern-intelligence; it is a claim on them." Nothing in DP touches this.
- **Coordination as a cost channel and the community as a learner.** The rig design finds that "least-time at two scales falls straight out of the table" and that "a community is a learner," its trajectory a learning curve in participation time. [ASSEMBLER] This is CE finding Least-Time Learning from its own side, six days after DP ruled the name. It is the strongest evidence in either repo that the root is real.

## 4. Where they would disagree if pushed

These are not errors. Each is a place where the two branches have made different moves on the same question, and the difference is load-bearing.

### 4.1 Is the shared quantity energy × time, or time?

DP ruled, 2026-08-24, that the name is Least-Time, Fermat not Hamilton, and that the minimised quantity is the learner's effort-time. §4.13 calls this "a naming ruling, not a retraction of §4.2" and says the action functional "is the same quantity." CE's keystone is denominated in action, "energy × time," and says "hardness is a magnitude" of action.

Pushed, they are not the same quantity. Fermat's light minimises time through a medium of varying index; Hamilton's particle makes action stationary. In DP's learning domain the learner's effort is the index and time is what is minimised, so the thing that varies along the path is time. In CE's R(C, B, T) the exergy budget B and the horizon T are two separate bounds on the reachable set, not a product. CE's rig then finds its natural unit is participation time, which is Fermat's quantity, not Hamilton's.

My read: the root is the shape, not the units. What is shared is a cost functional over paths in a configuration space with an extremal that has to be searched for. Whether the integrand is energy, effort or one is a choice each branch makes for its own domain, and DP has made it and CE has not. CE's keystone should say action where it means the physics analogy and time where it means what a coordinating community actually spends. Until it does, the two branches read as sharing a functional they do not share.

### 4.2 Is the reachable set observer-independent?

CE's formal setup defines R(C, B, T) as the configurations "reachable from C using exergy ≤ B in time ≤ T, under physical and biophysical constraints." That is a third-person set. Value is then made observer-relative by slicing it to R_living. The floor, `binding-constraint`, is `derived`, and what it is derived from, `exergy-not-energy`, is `established` on classical thermodynamics with no observer in it at all.

DP ruled on 2026-07-14 that "there is no coherence to the question 'reality' as actually is" and that ontology itself is an OLU construct. Under that ruling there is no observer-independent reachable set for CE to slice. There is only what bounded persistent observers can distinguish as reachable.

Pushed: DP says the floor is where observation ends; CE says the floor is where physics ends, whether or not anyone observes it. CE needs the second reading, because a floor that is negotiable by observers is not a floor, and the whole point of `binding-constraint` is that it cannot be priced away. DP's ruling makes that floor an agreement among OLUs rather than a fact about the world.

My read: this is a real disagreement and it should stand. CE is right to keep its floor observer-free, because its argument has to survive readers who reject DP entirely. DP is right that the floor, like everything else, is stated by an observer. The reconciliation, if there is one, is DP's own: the floor is the one agreement no OLU can revise, which is what "the denominator that does not move" already says. But nobody has written that sentence into CE, and CE's `established` label on the exergy grounding would have to become "established for OLUs" to carry it. Not a change to make quietly.

### 4.3 Is DP required by CE, or optional?

CE ruled in April 2026: "decouple rhetorically, couple optionally... CE survives if DP drifts." The lineage page says the same. CE's observer-architecture note, July 2026, says DP's primitives "upgrade the foundation's job in this project from preferred vocabulary to observer theory of the world computer." DP's front page, August 2026, puts ethics on the trinity as "selection over configurations, denominated against heat death," which is CE's entire subject matter stated as a leg of DP.

So DP now claims CE as its ethics leg, CE's ruling says it stands without DP, and CE's live note says DP is its observer theory. This is CANON C4 and it has not been ruled.

Pushed: DP's trinity says a curriculum is "a selection over configurations of a person's network" and CE says an economy is a selection over configurations of a world. If both are true then CE is DP's ethics at a larger scale and cannot also be independent of it. If CE is independent, then DP's ethics leg is a claim DP makes about itself that its sibling has declined.

My read, and this is the one I would ask Tom to rule on: the branches do not share the root by inheritance. They share it by convergence. The same person hit the same shape twice, from learning in 2025 and from accounting in 2026, and the texts show it: CE's keystone quotes DP, but CE's rig, its maintenance cost, its observer class and its floor were each found from the economics side and then recognised. That is a stronger relationship than inheritance, and it is the honest one. It also dissolves C4: CE keeps its firewall for the argument, because an argument that stands on its own is worth more than one that stands on a sibling, and DP keeps CE as its ethics leg, because that is a claim about what DP is for, not a claim CE has to co-sign. The observer-architecture note is right about the deepening and should say "for the deepening" in so many words.

### 4.4 Minimum, or stationary?

DP §4.13 leaves this open by name: "In physics, action is stationary, not always minimal. If that carries over, there exist learning paths that are locally unimprovable yet globally beaten by a reroute... This edge is left open deliberately and is not to be closed by paraphrase."

CE's keystone treats pattern-intelligence as finding "the short way through," a minimiser. CE's viable objective, meanwhile, already refused the scalar optimum: no-regret over moves, not maximisation over states, "closer to first do no harm than maximise this number."

Pushed: CE's ethics has taken the position DP's learning theory says it is not ready to take. The viable objective is the search-not-optimisation doctrine applied to value. The keystone still speaks the optimisation language DP flagged.

My read: CE's viable objective is the more advanced position and DP should read it back. The learning-theory edge left open in §4.13 may already be answered in CE: the right rule is not "find the minimum" but "reject every move that forecloses," which is a no-regret rule over paths and does not require the extremal to be a minimum. Not closing the DP edge by paraphrase; naming that its sibling has a candidate answer.

### 4.5 Whose budget?

DP's cost is one learner's seconds. CE's budget is a system's exergy over a horizon, spent on behalf of a class. DP's ethics denominator is one observer's finite budget against heat death. CE's is a class's persistence. Neither branch has an aggregation rule. CE says so, and made the refusal a feature: the viable objective "does not require" one. DP does not say so, because DP has never needed one: the learner is one person.

Pushed: the moment DP's ethics leg is applied to more than one observer, which the trinity's wording invites, it inherits CE's hole. My read: this is not a disagreement, it is a shared gap, and the right thing is to name it in both places rather than let DP's front page imply an ethics it has not built. "Selection over configurations, denominated against heat death" is a denominator. It is not yet a selection rule for more than one person's configurations.

## 5. The one-line relation, proposed

The distinction thesis is the theory of what one bounded, persistent observer can hold and at what cost. Configuration Economics is the theory of what a class of such observers should select, given that cost. The root is the cost of holding a distinction over time, against a floor that does not move, and the fact that the cheap path has to be found.

That line is mine, not Tom's, and it is the thing this node is for. If it is wrong, it is wrong in a way that can be said in one sentence.

## 7. What this node asks of Tom

Three rulings, each answerable in a sentence. My recommendation is stated on each.

1. **Convergence, not inheritance.** Is the relationship between the branches best described as two arrivals at one root, rather than CE as DP's application? Recommend yes. It is what the texts show, and it settles C4 without either side giving anything up.
2. **Time or action, in CE.** Should CE's keystone follow DP's naming ruling and say time where it means what is spent? Recommend yes, but only in the keystone and the rig; the §4.2 bridge in the formalisation draft can keep action, because there it is the physics analogy and nothing else.
3. **The floor's label.** Does CE's `established` floor survive DP's ontology ruling as written, or does it need a line saying it is the one agreement no observer can revise? Recommend the line, in CE, in Tom's words, because without it the two branches contradict each other on the one thing both say cannot move.

Everything else in §4 is stated as a position and can wait.
